      🎯 Start here

      Before you read “My Life with the Chimpanzees,” think about its author, Jane Goodall. A KWL chart helps you plan and track your learning.

      What is a KWL chart?

      K: What I KnowThings I already know or think I know.
      W: What I Want to knowQuestions I am curious about.
      L: What I LearnedAnswers and new ideas I find after reading.
      1. 🌱 Before you read: fill in the K and W columns.
      2. 📖 While you read: look for answers to your questions.
      3. 🔍 After you read: fill in the L column and check your K notes.
      4. 📸 Turn it in: take a screenshot of your chart and upload it to Canvas.

      🔊 Tap the speaker to hear any part read aloud. ✏️ You can type notes or write and draw with your Apple Pencil. Your work saves on this iPad.

      🌱 K: What I already know

      What do I know about Jane Goodall, chimpanzees, or scientists who study animals?

      Write one idea at a time. It is okay if you are not sure. You will check these notes after you read.

      Write or draw here with your Apple Pencil. Your teacher can use this box for a class chart.

      ❓ W: What I want to know

      What questions do I have?

      Good questions start with Who, What, Where, When, Why, or How. You will look for the answers while you read.

      Write or draw here with your Apple Pencil.

      💡 L: What I learned

      Do this after you read “My Life with the Chimpanzees.” Use your own words.

      1. Answer my questions

      Look at your W questions. Tap I found the answer and write what you learned. Still curious? Look in reliable sources, like you practiced on Day 3.

      2. Check what I knew

      Look at your K notes. Was each one right?

      3. More things I learned

      Write or draw here with your Apple Pencil.
